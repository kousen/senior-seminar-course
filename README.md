# CPSC 403: Computer Science Seminar, Fall 2026

Course materials for Ken Kousen's senior seminar at Trinity College. The seminar is about presenting technical work, built around Nancy Duarte's *Resonate*.

- [Syllabus](syllabus.md), including the weekly schedule
- Assignment sheets:
  - [Presentation guidelines](assignments/presentation-guidelines.md) for both talks and the faculty pitch
  - [Talk analysis](assignments/talk-analysis.md), due September 22
  - [Post-mortem](assignments/post-mortem.md), due one week after each of your talks
  - [Absence memo](assignments/absence-memo.md), due before the next class after any absence
- `slides/` will hold the chapter decks as they are produced

Moodle holds announcements, submissions, and grades. The presentation sign-up sheet is linked from the Moodle course page.

Textbook: *Resonate: Present Visual Stories that Transform Audiences* by Nancy Duarte (Wiley, 2010). A [free online edition](https://www.resonatebook.com/) is available with registration.
