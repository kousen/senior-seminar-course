# Post-Mortem (after each of your two talks)

**Due:** one week after your own talk. The Moodle due date on each round (October 27 for Presentation 1, December 1 for Presentation 2) is the outer limit for everyone. Individual, even if you presented with a partner or a team. **Pass/redo.**

## Why this exists

Chapter 8 of *Resonate* is about screening a talk with honest critics and improving it. You just gave a talk to a room full of them. Write down what happened while you still remember it.

## One page, four parts

1. **What I intended.** What did you want the audience to believe or do afterward? What was the plan for getting them there?
2. **What landed.** Which moments worked? How do you know: a laugh, a question, faces, the feedback you got afterward?
3. **What did not.** Which moments fell flat, ran long, or confused people? Be concrete. Timing counts: if you went over ten minutes, say where the time went.
4. **What I will change.** Two or three specific things you will do differently next time. Not "practice more." Something a classmate could check for in your next talk.

## Post-Mortem 2 adds two comparisons

- **Against Post-Mortem 1.** Look at what you said you would change. Did you? What happened?
- **Against your talk analysis.** Reread the September write-up. Would you critique that speaker the same way now? What do you notice in your own talk that you did not notice in theirs?

## Pass or redo

A pass is honest, specific to your talk, and grounded in what actually happened in the room. Generic reflections ("I was nervous, next time I'll be more confident") come back for a redo. This is the one assignment in the course that an AI cannot write for you, because it was not there.
