# Absence Memo

**Due:** before the next class after any class you miss. **Pass/redo.**

## Why this exists

The seminar meets once a week. Miss one meeting and you have missed a large slice of the semester and, on presentation days, a classmate's talk. Recordings are posted on Zoom, but very few people watch them unprompted. The memo makes sure you do, and it is what turns an unexcused absence into an excused one.

## What to write

One page maximum.

- **Key points covered.** What was discussed, which chapter, what came up in questions.
- **Action items.** Anything assigned, any deadline mentioned, anything you now owe someone.
- **One line that is yours.** The most important takeaway and why you think so, *or* a question you would have asked if you had been there.

**If the class you missed was a presentation day**, you may instead write one page of feedback to the students who presented, based on the recording. Use the same vocabulary you use in class. I will pass it along to them.

## AI

Using an AI tool to summarize the recording is fine and in the spirit of the course. The personal line is the part that has to be you.

## Pass or redo

A memo that is accurate and includes a real personal line passes. A memo that is only a summary, or that clearly did not come from the recording, comes back once.

**No memo means the absence counts** against Attendance and Participation.

## Submit

Upload a file to the Absence Memos assignment in the Course Information section of Moodle. Name the file with the date of the class you missed and leave earlier memos in place.
